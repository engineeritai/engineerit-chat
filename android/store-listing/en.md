# Play Store listing — English

App name (11/30): engineer it

Short description (74/80): Engineering chat, tools, and advisors. Free and paid plans in Saudi Riyal.

Full description:

engineer it is the Android app for engineerit.ai, an AI-assisted engineering workspace. You chat about engineering topics, review drawings and documents, and use project tools. The replies are drafts. A qualified engineer still has to check them before they are used.

What you can do
- Chat across engineering disciplines
- Use tools for drawings and diagrams, design checks, inspection and quality plans, quantities, schedules, value engineering, and project dashboards
- Open advisors on the plans that include them

Plans, in Saudi Riyal, paid through Moyasar
- Assistant Engineer: free
- Engineer: SAR 19 per month, or SAR 190 per year
- Professional Engineer: SAR 41 per month, or SAR 410 per year
- Consultant Engineer: SAR 99 per month, or SAR 990 per year. Includes the PMO Advisor
- Team: SAR 449 per month, or SAR 4,490 per year, for 5 seats. An extra seat is SAR 89 per month. Includes PMO and Program Assurance
- Enterprise: SAR 3,900 per month, or SAR 39,000 per year, for 10 seats. An extra seat is SAR 299 per month. Includes PMO, Program Assurance, Operations Readiness, and Decision Support

The yearly price is 10 times the monthly price. VAT applies as required by the rules in force. Moyasar processes the card payment. engineerit.ai does not store your card number.

An account uses your email address. Chats and files you upload are kept so you can open them again. Paid plans can be cancelled from billing.
